# Resonance

Resonance occurs in an [LC circuit](01_Reactive_Combinations.md) ([Inductor](../02_components/09_Inductors.md) + [Capacitor](../02_components/05_Capacitors.md)) when Inductive [Reactance](../02_components/07_Reactance.md) equals Capacitive [Reactance](../02_components/07_Reactance.md) ($X_L = X_C$).

## Resonant Frequency ($f_{res}$)
Calculated using the **Thomson Formula**:
$$f_{res} = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{L \cdot C}}$$

## Series Resonance
*   **[Impedance](../01_electricity/22_Impedance.md)**: Minimal ($Z = R_{loss} \approx 0$).
*   **Behavior**: Acts as a **short circuit** at resonance.
*   **Application**: Suction circuit (Zuigkring) to shunt interference to ground.

## Parallel Resonance
*   **[Impedance](../01_electricity/22_Impedance.md)**: Maximal ($Z$ is very high).
*   **Behavior**: Acts as an **open circuit** at resonance.
*   **Application**: Tuned circuit (Kring), Trap (Sperkring).
