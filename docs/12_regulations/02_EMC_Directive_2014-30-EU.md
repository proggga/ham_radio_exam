# EMC Directive (2014/30/EU)

The **EMC Directive** is European legislation ensuring that electronic equipment does not generate electromagnetic disturbance and is not affected by such disturbance.

## Key Goals
1.  **Emission**: Equipment must not generate electromagnetic interference (EMI) that prevents other equipment (radio, TV) from functioning.
2.  **Immunity**: Equipment must be able to operate normally in the presence of expected electromagnetic disturbance.

## Amateur Radio Exemption (Exam Topic)
*   **Commercially Available Gear**: Must comply with the directive and bear the **CE Mark**.
*   **Homebrew (Zelfbouw)**: Equipment built by radio amateurs for their own use is **EXEMPT** from the EMC Directive.
    *   *Condition:* It is used by amateurs and not placed on the open market.
    *   *Modified Gear:* Commercially modified equipment used by amateurs is also exempt.

## CE Marking
*   Indicates conformity with all relevant EU directives (EMC, Low Voltage, RoHS).
*   Required for any equipment sold in the EU.
