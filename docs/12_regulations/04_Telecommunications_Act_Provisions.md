# Telecommunicatiewet Provisions

The **Telecommunicatiewet** (Telecommunications Act) is the primary legislation governing all radio and wire communications in the Netherlands.

## Key Articles (Exam Requirements)
*   **Article 1.1 (Definitions)**:
    *   **"Onze Minister"**: The Minister of Economic Affairs (Minister van Economische Zaken), responsible for spectrum management (via [RDI](05_Licensing_in_the_Netherlands.md)).
    *   **"Radioapparaat"**: Any device geared towards sending or receiving radio waves.
*   **Article 15.3 (Interference & Enforcement)**:
    *   Gives the RDI authority to impose an immediate **Transmission Ban (Zendverbod)** if a station causes inadmissible interference or poses a safety risk, even if the equipment technically meets standards.
