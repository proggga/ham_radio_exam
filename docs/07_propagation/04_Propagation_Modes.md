# Map: Propagation Modes

This map covers the different paths radio waves take to travel from transmitter to receiver.

## Standard Modes
*   Ground Wave Propagation - LF/MF, following the surface.
*   Sky Wave Propagation - [HF](01_Propagation_Basics.md), skipping off the ionosphere.
*   Line of Sight Propagation (LOS) - [VHF](15_VHFUHF_Bands_6m,_2m,_70cm.md)/[UHF](15_VHFUHF_Bands_6m,_2m,_70cm.md), direct path.

## Extended VHF/UHF Modes
*   Tropospheric Propagation - Ducting and scattering due to weather.
*   Exotic Propagation Modes - [Aurora](07_Special_Propagation_Modes.md), [Meteor Scatter](07_Special_Propagation_Modes.md), [EME](../11_procedures.md).
