# Map: Propagation Modes

This map covers the different paths radio waves take to travel from transmitter to receiver.

## Standard Modes
*   [Ground Wave Propagation](05_Ground_Wave_Propagation.md) - LF/MF, following the surface.
*   [Sky Wave Propagation](06_Sky_Wave_Propagation.md) - [HF](01_Propagation_Basics.md), skipping off the ionosphere.
*   [Line of Sight Propagation (LOS)](07_Line_of_Sight_Propagation_LOS.md) - [VHF](08_VHFUHF_Bands_6m,_2m,_70cm.md)/[UHF](08_VHFUHF_Bands_6m,_2m,_70cm.md), direct path.

## Extended VHF/UHF Modes
*   [Tropospheric Propagation](09_Tropospheric_Propagation.md) - Ducting and scattering due to weather.
*   [Exotic Propagation Modes](10_Exotic_Propagation_Modes.md) - [Aurora](11_Special_Propagation_Modes.md), [Meteor Scatter](11_Special_Propagation_Modes.md), [EME](../11_procedures.md).
