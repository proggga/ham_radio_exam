# Antenna Types

This map covers the various types of antennas used in amateur radio.

## Wire Antennas
*   **[The Dipole Antenna](02_The_Dipole_Antenna.md)** - The reference standard ($\lambda/2$).
*   **[End-Fed Antenna](03_End-Fed_Antenna.md)** - End-Fed Half Wave (EFHW) and Zepp.
*   **[Multiband Antennas](04_Multiband_Antennas.md)** - Traps and Fan dipoles.
*   **[Common Wire Antennas](05_Common_Wire_Antennas.md)** - G5RV, Windom, OCFD.
*   **[Loop Antennas](06_Loop_Antennas.md)** - Magnetic and Full-wave loops.

## Vertical
*   **[Vertical Antennas](07_Vertical_Antennas.md)** - Ground planes and monopoles.
*   **[J-Pole Antenna](08_J-Pole_Antenna.md)** - End-fed vertical for VHF/UHF.
*   **[Discone Antenna](09_Discone_Antenna.md)** - Wideband scanner antenna.
*   **[Mobile, Portable & Marine Antennas](10_Mobile,_Portable_&_Marine_Antennas.md)** - Shortened and loaded antennas.
*   **[Hidden and Limited-Space Antennas](11_Hidden_and_Limited-Space_Antennas.md)** - Compromise solutions.

## Directional
*   **[Directional Antennas (Beams)](12_Directional_Antennas_Beams.md)** - [Yagi](12_Directional_Antennas_Beams.md)-Uda and Quads for gain.
*   **[Aperture Antennas](13_Aperture_Antennas.md)** - Dishes and Helical antennas for [UHF](../07_propagation/15_VHFUHF_Bands_6m,_2m,_70cm.md)/Microwave.
*   **[Microwave Waveguides and Antennas](14_Microwave_Waveguides_and_Antennas.md)** - High frequency transmission.

## Special
*   **[Dummy Load](15_Dummy_Load.md)** - Non-radiating load for testing.
