# Dutch Frequency Regulations 2015

The **Regeling gebruik frequentieruimte met meldingsplicht 2015** is the specific regulation that details *how* amateurs may use the frequency spectrum.

## Key Provisions
*   **Frequency Tables**: Defines exactly which bands (e.g., [[VHFUHF Bands (6m, 2m, 70cm)|2m Band]], [[40m Band]]) are available.
*   **Power Limits**: Sets the maximum transmission power (PEP) for different license classes (N and F).
*   **Status**: Defines Primary vs. Secondary status per band.
*   **User Categories**: Distinguishes between Full (F) and Novice (N) users.

## Relation to Other Laws
*   Derived from the **[[Telecommunicatiewet Provisions|Telecommunicatiewet]]** and **Frequentiebesluit**.
*   Implemented via the **[[Licensing in the Netherlands|Registration]]** system.
