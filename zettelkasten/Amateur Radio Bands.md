# Amateur Radio Bands

Guide to the characteristics and usage of the different amateur radio bands.

## Power Limits (Netherlands)
*   **F-[[Licensing in the Netherlands|Registration]] (Full)**:
    *   **HF**: Generally **400 Watts** PEP (Exceptions: 60m is 15W EIRP).
    *   **VHF/UHF**: **400 Watts** PEP on 2m/70cm. **120W** on 6m.
*   **N-Registration (Novice)** (Since June 2021):
    *   **HF**: **100 Watts** PEP on permitted bands (40m, 20m, 10m).
    *   **VHF/UHF**: **25 Watts** PEP on 2m and 70cm.
    *   *Permitted Bands:* 40m, 20m, 10m, 2m, 70cm.
    *   *Forbidden Bands:* 160m, 80m, 60m, 30m, 17m, 12m, 6m, 4m, 23cm & up.

## HF Bands (Low & Mid)
*   **[[160m Band (Top Band)]]** ([[160m Band (Top Band)|Top Band]]) - Night/Winter DX.
*   **[[80m Band]]** - Regional workhorse. Not for N-amateurs.
*   **[[40m Band]]** - Reliable 24/7. Shared status (Secondary in some regions).
*   **[[20m Band]]** - Premier DX band.

## HF Bands (High)
*   **[[10m Band]]** - Solar max powerhouse / [[Special Propagation Modes|Sporadic E]].
*   **[[WARC Bands (30m, 17m, 12m)]]** - [[WARC Bands (30m, 17m, 12m)|30m, 17m, 12m]] (Contest-free).

## VHF/UHF
*   **[[VHFUHF Bands (6m, 2m, 70cm)]]** - [[VHFUHF Bands (6m, 2m, 70cm)|6m, 2m, 70cm]] (Line of Sight, [[Repeater Operation|Repeater]], Tropo).
    *   **6m (50 MHz)**: "Magic Band".
    *   **2m (144 MHz)**: Most popular local band. 400W allowed for F.
    *   **70cm (430 MHz)**: Shared with other services (LPD, Car keys). Secondary status often applies.
