# Reactance & Impedance

This map covers AC circuit theory regarding resistance to current flow.

## Fundamentals
*   **[[Phase Relationships]]** - Lead and lag in L and C circuits.
*   **[[Reactance]]** - Frequency dependent resistance ($X_L, X_C$).
*   **[[Impedance]]** - Total opposition ($Z$) and vector calculation.
*   **[[Admittance]]** - The inverse of Impedance ($Y$), useful for parallel circuits.
