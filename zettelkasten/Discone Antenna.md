# Discone Antenna

The **Discone** (Disc + Cone) is a popular omnidirectional wideband antenna, primarily used for scanning and wide-spectrum monitoring.

## Construction
*   **Disc**: A flat metal disc (or array of rods) on top. Acts as the capacitive top load.
*   **Cone**: A metal cone (or skirt of rods) pointing downwards. Acts as the ground plane.
*   **Feed**: The coax center conductor connects to the Disc, and the shield connects to the Cone.

## Characteristics
*   **Bandwidth**: Extremely wide. Typical frequency range ratio is **10:1** (e.g., 25 MHz to 1300 MHz).
*   **Impedance**: Constant $\approx 50 \Omega$ across the range.
*   **Gain**: **Unity (0 dBd)**. It does *not* provide gain over a dipole.
*   **Pattern**: Omnidirectional, similar to a Ground Plane.
*   **Polarization**: Vertical.

## Applications
*   **Scanners**: Ideal for listening to Police, Airband, Marine, and Ham bands simultaneously.
*   **Transmit**: Can be used for transmitting on amateur bands (6m, 2m, 70cm, 23cm) within its frequency range due to low [[Standing Wave Ratio (SWR)|SWR]].
    *   *Note:* It is not optimized for transmitting efficiency (low angle radiation varies), but it is safe for the radio.
